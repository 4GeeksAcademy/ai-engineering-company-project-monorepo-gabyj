# Project Brief

## Business Overview

TrackFlow is a last-mile logistics and warehouse management company founded in 2009. It operates in the United States and Spain, with warehouses in Los Angeles and Zaragoza, approximately 130 employees, and annual revenue of around 9 million euros. It stores inventory for e-commerce brands, prepares and ships their orders through carriers, and manages returns.

TrackFlow Tech is the internal unit responsible for building the systems, integrations, and intelligent automations needed to modernize and scale operations.

## Problem the Project Solves

Operations rely on fragmented systems and manual processes: the two warehouses use different tools and have no shared inventory visibility; orders, shipments, and returns involve repetitive manual tasks; carrier data is not structured; customer support lacks a unified platform and knowledge base; and executive reports are compiled manually from outdated data.

This leads to delays, errors, avoidable costs, and decisions based on incomplete information. The operational gap is widening as competitors automate their processes.

## Project Objectives

- Unify visibility into and access to operational data across the United States and Spain.
- Automate workflows for orders, inventory, carrier selection and tracking, returns, and customer support.
- Provide up-to-date dashboards and reports for operations, customers, and leadership.
- Apply AI where it adds value: image-based return classification, semantic search, customer assistance, and explainable carrier recommendations.
- Improve the reliability, efficiency, and scalability of logistics operations while keeping services available for continuous operations.
